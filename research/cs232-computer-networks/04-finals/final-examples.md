R1 (5Points) Consider two random variables X1 and X2, distributed according to an Exponential distribution with
parameters λ1 and λ2. The expectation of the random variable X= min(X1, X2) is
a)
λ1 + λ2;
b)
min(λ1, λ2);
c)
1/(λ1 + λ2);
d)
1/ min(λ1, λ2);
R2 (XPoints) Describe multiplexing and demultiplexing of TCP and UDP protocols.
R3 (XPoints) Describe the slotted Aloha protocol. Does the protocol provide adaptation to congestion? Explain
your answer.
R4 (XPoints) Discuss why hierarchical routing is used in the internet (instead of ﬂat 1-level routing).
R4 (XPoints) Describe the operations of the slow start and congestion avoidance states in TCP.
