## Page 13

Sensors and Actuators
´ Sensors: input components
´ Passive, omnidirectional (e.g., mic)
´ Passive, narrow-beam sensor (e.g., PIR)
´ Active sensors (e.g., sonar, lidar, and radar)
´ Actuators: output components
´ Adding lighting, heat, sound, etc.
´ Controlling motors to move objects
´ Displaying messages
´ A “thing” generally consists of four main parts:
´ Sensors & actuators
´ Microcontroller
´ Network interfaces
´ Power supply
