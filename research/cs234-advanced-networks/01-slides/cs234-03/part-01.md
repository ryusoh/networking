## Page 5

What is Cloud Platform?
´ a new class of network based computing
that takes place over the Internet
´ was referred to as utility computing earlier
´ cloud platform: a bunch of integrated and
networked hardware, software and Internet
infrastructure
´ delivers hardware, software and networking
services over the Internet to clients
´ Cloud platforms hide the complexity and
details from users and applications by
providing simple API or even graphical
interface
5
