## Page 43

Additional Challenges
´ More flexible bitrate allocations
´ The presented work only considers a few quality levels
´ What is a good quality (QoE) metrics?
´ Subjects can rotate their heads anyway they like…
´ Content may be very different from one another:
Tourism versus extreme sports; single or multiple
objects?
´ How to guide viewers? Compare against the
traditional movie director…
´ Video stabilization and stitching ß images are
from multiple cameras
´ Current 360 videos only support 3 Degree-of-
Freedom (DoF), how to support 3DoF+ and 6DoF?
´ Can we support multiple focal planes (e.g., with
lightfield videos) ß important for AR glasses
´ Anything else? I guess content is the key!

## Page 44

Take-Away Messages
´ 360° video technology is an interesting area with
many  new challenges.
´ Fundamentally 360° videos require a tremendous
´ amount of resources (e.g., bandwidth, storage).
´ Some of the existing techniques can be adapted,
for example MPEG-DASH, but many parts need to
be optimized for a high quality user experience.
´ Much interesting work still needs to be done!
´ Also, new (more exciting) content types, such as
point clouds and lightfield images are around the
corner!
