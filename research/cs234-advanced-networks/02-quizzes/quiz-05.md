OOB Mechanism: Utilizing two separate networks or channels,
one of which is differ-ent from the primary network or
channel, to simultaneously communicate between two parties or
devices for identifying a user.
Example: A cellular network is commonly used for OOB
authentication. An example of OOB authentication is when an
online-banking user is accessing her online bank account with
a login and a one-time password (OTP) is sent to her mobile
phone via SMS (short message service).
Resource Manager use a dynamic lease mechanism to help master
nodes to compute the estimated time before assigning the
volunteer nodes, thus ensure fault tolerance.
If churn happens after the assignment then replication will
solve the problem. The master node would have copies of either
the data or the computation and will resort to them.
